# Q-03 Phone corner controls

Status: built, awaiting review and gate
Branch: `polish-q03-phone-corner-controls`

## What was wrong (375x812, v2 Lantern Road, Oskmark)

The scale bar sat on the minimap's bottom-left. Reset sat on the minimap's corner (phone and
desktop: both were `bottom-3 right-3`). With the ruler on, the hint "Click or tap two points to
measure" lay under Wander, the scale bar and the minimap, and "Done measuring" floated alone at the
top right. On desktop the hint also overlapped the scale bar. Tap targets: zoom 30x30, Reset 32x32,
the pin Legend 22 px tall, Wander 38 px, the toolbar's ruler, grid and calm buttons 36 px.

## What moved where

Placement now lives in named classes in `src/index.css` ("Map corner controls (Q-03)") instead of
position utilities on each element.

- **Reset** (`.atlas-reset-view`): under the zoom buttons, top-left, on every width. Desktop 34x34
  (the zoom bar's outer width), touch 44x44.
- **Scale bar** (`.atlas-scale-bar`): from 640 px unchanged (bottom centre). Below 640 px it sits
  above Wander at the left, `bottom: 82px`. The notice dock's phone clearance now counts it
  (`noticeDockClearancePx(minimap, credits, scaleBar)`, `PHONE_SCALE_BAR_BOTTOM_PX` in
  `src/atlas/noticeDockLayout.ts`), so a short minimap can't let the dock drop onto it.
- **Ruler hint** (`.atlas-ruler-hint`, `RulerLayer` lost its inline offsets): top centre from 768 px;
  below 768 px, 60 px down (under the top row); with "Done measuring" on screen (v2 phone bar), right
  under that button, right-aligned, as one group (`#atlas-main:has(.atlas-ruler-done)`; without
  `:has` support it falls back to top centre).
- **Done measuring** (`.atlas-ruler-done`): top right, 60 px down, under the 40 px Legend.
- **Canvas legend** ("map: zoom in or click"): 64 px in on touch widths, clear of the wider zoom bar.
- **Touch sizes** (`@media (max-width: 1023px), (pointer: coarse)`): zoom buttons 40x40, Reset
  44x44, pin Legend 40 px tall, Wander 40 px, the toolbar's grid, ruler, calm and search buttons
  40 px tall (`.atlas-tool-button`). `WANDER_HEIGHT_PX` is now 62 (the phone's Wander column).

## Desktop (1440x900, mouse) differences

- Reset moved from the bottom-right corner (on the minimap) to under the zoom buttons, and is
  34x34 instead of 32x32.
- The ruler hint moved from bottom centre (over the scale bar) to top centre.
- Nothing else: zoom, Legend, Wander and toolbar sizes are as before.

## Measurements (bounding boxes, JS, headless Chromium)

Overlaps among minimap, scale bar, Reset, zoom in/out, Wander, canvas legend, pin Legend, ruler
hint, Done measuring and every notice-dock child: **none**, at 375x812, 360x640, 812x375 and
1440x900, ruler off and on, v2 (Lantern Road, Oskmark) and v1 (`atlas:publish` + `vite preview`,
Astrath overview). With a stand-in update toast injected into the dock (same classes as
`UpdateNotice`) and the ruler on: none at all four sizes (v2). Nothing under the toolbar.
