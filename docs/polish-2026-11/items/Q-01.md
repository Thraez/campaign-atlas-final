# Q-01 One ocean view

Branch `polish-q01-one-ocean-view` (from `main`). 2026-11-25. v2 shared canvas only.

First load, the Ocean crumb, Reset and the zoom-out floor (wheel, keyboard, pinch, zoom buttons)
now all end on the same camera: the P-02 ocean fit (`oceanFitBox`, every top-level map whole with
`OCEAN_FIT_MARGIN`, clear of the side panel).

## Causes

1. **The Ocean crumb went to memory.** `AtlasViewer` called `goView(ocean, "memory")`, which
   returns to the last camera seen on the ocean: after wheeling into a map that is the handoff
   point (zoom about -0.1 at 1440x900), with Quessaly filling the screen and Skerrow off it.
2. **The floor sat one level below the fit.** R-36's provisional `OCEAN_ZOOM_OUT_MARGIN = 1`, so
   the wheel went past the fit to a smaller, off-centre ocean (-2.5 against the fit's -1.5).
3. **Nothing re-fitted.** A resize (window, side panel) only moved the floor, and a zoom that
   reached the floor stayed wherever the cursor anchored it.

The reported "wheel stops at -0.2" and "Reset is larger than first load" did not reproduce: the
wheel always reached the floor, and Reset matched first load. The likeliest source of the second
is a first-load fit made at a different container size that no resize re-fitted (cause 3).

## What changed

- `OceanZoomLimit.tsx`: `OCEAN_ZOOM_OUT_MARGIN` 0, so the floor is the fit zoom (R-36's provisional
  "one level below" is retired). A zoom that settles at the floor pans to the fit centre
  (`GLIDE_SECONDS`, a jump in calm mode). A resize while at the floor re-fits without animation.
  Both clear ZoomHandoff's `armed` flag, so neither hands off.
- `AtlasCanvas.tsx`: passes the fit centre, view height, calm mode and `armed` to it.
  `canvasGeometry.ts`: `boxCentre`.
- `AtlasViewer.tsx`: the Ocean crumb goes to `"fit"` with a glide; map crumbs still remember.
