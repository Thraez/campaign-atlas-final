# Q-01 One ocean view

Branch `polish-q01-one-ocean-view` (from `main`). 2026-11-25. v2 shared canvas only.

First load, the Ocean crumb, Reset and the zoom-out floor (wheel, keyboard, pinch, zoom buttons)
now all end on the same camera: the P-02 ocean fit (`oceanFitBox`, every top-level map whole with
`OCEAN_FIT_MARGIN`, clear of the side panel).

## Causes

1. **The Ocean crumb went to memory.** `AtlasViewer` called `goView(ocean, "memory")`, which
   returns to the last camera seen on the ocean: after wheeling into a map that is the handoff
   point (zoom about -0.1 at 1440x900), with Quessaly filling the screen and Skerrow off it.
2. **The floor sat one level below the fit.** R-36's provisional `OCEAN_ZOOM_OUT_MARGIN = 1`, so
   the wheel went past the fit to a smaller, off-centre ocean (-2.5 against the fit's -1.5).
3. **Nothing re-fitted.** A resize (window, side panel) only moved the floor, and a zoom that
   reached the floor stayed wherever the cursor anchored it.

The reported "wheel stops at -0.2" and "Reset is larger than first load" did not reproduce: the
wheel always reached the floor, and Reset matched first load. The likeliest source of the second
is a first-load fit made at a different container size that no resize re-fitted (cause 3).

## What changed

- `OceanZoomLimit.tsx`: `OCEAN_ZOOM_OUT_MARGIN` 0, so the floor is the fit zoom (R-36's provisional
  "one level below" is retired). A zoom that settles at the floor pans to the fit centre
  (`GLIDE_SECONDS`, a jump in calm mode). Every move below clears ZoomHandoff's `armed` flag, so
  none hands off.
- A slide cut short carries on. Leaflet stops a running pan on every wheel batch and every
  `setView` (`map._stop()`: moveend, no zoomend), so trackpad inertia or a held "-" used to leave
  the camera at the floor zoom but off the fit. On a moveend while sliding, next tick, if no pan is
  running (`_panAnim._inProgress`), the camera is at the floor and off-centre, and no drag is
  under way, the slide starts again from there (at most 100 times per slide, a loop guard). A drag
  ends the slide.
- Resizes never animate. At the floor a resize re-fits (new floor, fit centre). That holds even
  after a deliberate drag at the floor: at the floor the camera is the fit, so opening or closing
  the side panel puts a dragged camera back on the fit centre. Away from the floor the camera
  stays; if the floor rises above it, the zoom is raised and the slide that follows is a jump.
- A map hidden at mount runs the floor setup once when first shown (the resize listener is added
  after `whenMapHasSize`'s first run, not beside it).
- `AtlasCanvas.tsx`: passes the fit centre, view height, calm mode and `armed` to it.
  `canvasGeometry.ts`: `boxCentre`.
- `AtlasViewer.tsx`: the Ocean crumb goes to `"fit"` with a glide; map crumbs still remember.

Review fixes, rechecked in a fresh profile (the site's service worker otherwise serves the old
bundle): zoomed in to -0.5, cursor moved off-centre, one notch out to the floor then 20 small wheel
events 50 ms apart. The slide was cut short and restarted 17 times and settled at 1450, 702, -1.5.
A held "-" (16 presses 60 ms apart) settled at 1450, 699, -1.5.
The first commit was not rerun this way in the browser.

The 2-5 unit `cy` differences are under one screen pixel at these zooms. Shots:
`shots/q01-before-desktop-crumb.jpg` and `shots/q01-after-desktop-crumb.jpg` (JPEG, like the
other items' shots). The "before" numbers come from a build of the parent commit.

Tests: `src/test/canvas/one-ocean-view.test.tsx` (19, fake map with the real fit maths: first
load, Reset, crumb, calm mode, wheel/keyboard/touch zoom-out, hand-back to the floor, resize at

hidden map shown once). The floor expectations in the R-36, camera, glide and canvas tests
moved from fit-1 to fit; the camera test's fake now runs at `zoomSnap` 0 like the canvas.

## Not checked, open

  keeps P-04's anchor). A short extra slide; judged fine, not playtested by a person.
